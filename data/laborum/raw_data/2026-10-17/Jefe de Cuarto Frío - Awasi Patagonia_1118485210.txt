DESCRIPCIÓN:
Awasi is a collection of three carbon-neutral all inclusive lodges that offer private programmes in some of Chile and Argentina's most iconic destinations: the Atacama Desert,Torres del Paine and Iguazu Falls.What sets us apart? We only have 12 to 14 rooms per hotel and one private guide and a 4WD are assigned per room, so the experience is as personalized and intimate as possible.From the comfort of our low-impact lodges to the expert knowledge of our trained guides, our team is passionate about making each guest’s adventure unique. We recognize time and space as the greatest of privileges and showcase the natural and cultural heritage of each location to reflect a strong sense of place.Awasi es una colección de tres hoteles all-inclusive y carbono neutrales que ofrecen programas privados en destinos icónicos de Chile y Argentina: el desierto de Atacama, Torres del Paine y las Cataratas de Iguazú.¿Que nos diferencia?
